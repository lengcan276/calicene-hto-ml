# Supplementary Dataset S2: conformer-resolved donor twist and signed energy gap

This dataset accompanies Section S8 and Table S8C. It contains existing frozen calculations; no new electronic-structure calculations were performed to prepare these tables.

## Files

- `S2_all159_inclusion.csv`: all 159 intended-species conformers, including unmeasurable geometries and inclusion flags.
- `S2_measured137.csv`: the 137 conformers with eligible measured donor groups.
- `S2_within134.csv`: the 134 measured conformers belonging to 32 parents with at least two measured conformers.
- `S2_donor_group_measurements.csv`: underlying donor-group records for the intended-species conformers, including ineligible groups. Eligible groups satisfy `kind != terminal` and `nitro_like == 0`. Their twist angles are averaged per conformer.
- `reproduce_S8.py`: portable reproduction of the correlation, permutation test and parent-direction summary. Run `python reproduce_S8.py` after installing NumPy and pandas. Tested versions are recorded in `reproduced_summary.json`.
- `reproduced_Table_S8C.csv`: unrounded parent-level results generated by the reproduction script.
- `reproduced_summary.json`: sample counts, test results, singleton identifiers and package versions.
- `source_manifest.json`: SHA-256 hashes and relative paths of the frozen input records and original script.
- `source_donor_twist_direct.py`: unchanged original geometry/statistical script, supplied for provenance. It retains its original project paths and dependencies; use `reproduce_S8.py` for portable reproduction from these tables.

## Column definitions

`parent_identifier` and `conformer_identifier` jointly identify a conformer. `analysis_order` is the zero-based row order inherited from the original model dataset. Preserve this ordering to reproduce the same finite sequence of seeded permutations.

`donor_twist_deg` is the arithmetic mean of eligible donor-group angles, in degrees. `S1_eV`, `T2_eV` and `gap_S1_T2_eV` give excitation energies and the signed difference E(S1)-E(T2), in eV. `HTO_label` is the canonical conformer label. `n_eligible_donor_groups` counts the groups used in the mean.

The binary flags `measurable`, `included_within_parent`, and `included_parent_direction_summary` respectively identify the 137 measurable conformers, the 134 within-parent analysis conformers and the 118 conformers in the 24-parent direction summary. `n_measured_conformers_in_parent` counts only measurable conformers of the parent. In this dataset all parents with at least three measured conformers have nonconstant angle and gap, as required for their Spearman coefficients.

`exclusion_reason` refers to inclusion in the pooled within-parent analysis: `included`, `singleton_measured_parent`, or `no_eligible_measured_donor_group`. There are three measured singletons and 22 conformers without eligible measured donor angles. Unavailable angles are blank, not zero. Singleton centered values are mathematically zero, but their inclusion flag is zero and they are excluded from the correlation.

`parent_mean_twist_deg` and `parent_mean_gap_eV_measured` use measurable conformers only. `centered_twist_deg` and `centered_gap_eV` subtract these respective parent means. Unmeasurable conformers have no centered pair. A parent mean can remain available on an unmeasurable row if other conformers of that parent were measured.

`model_source_csv_row`, `energy_source_csv_row` and `donor_source_csv_rows` identify one-based physical rows in the corresponding frozen CSVs listed in the manifest (header = row 1). Multiple donor-source rows are separated by semicolons. Floating-point values are serialized with 17 significant digits to preserve the stored numerical precision, not to imply that degree of physical accuracy.

## Statistical operation and results

Each variable is centered separately within parent. Pearson correlation is computed on the 134 pooled centered pairs. Centered angles are fixed; centered gaps are permuted within each parent. NumPy `default_rng(20260910)` generates 5,000 permutations. Exceedances satisfy abs(r_perm) >= abs(r_observed) - 1e-12; the two-sided p value is (exceedances + 1)/(5000 + 1).

The reproduced Pearson r is 0.35594994805708263, with zero exceedances and p = 0.0001999600079984003. The 24 parents with at least three measured conformers contain 118 conformers: 19 have positive and five negative Spearman correlations on the original uncentered pairs. This is a count of directions, not of individually statistically significant parents. Eight additional parents contribute two conformers each. The remaining three measurable conformers are singletons: `5ring_nh2_in/conf_1`, `5ring_nh2_3ring_cn_out/conf_1`, and `5ring_nh2_3ring_cn_in/conf_1`.

The reported twist range is minimum to maximum angle, not angular variance. These analyses quantify static geometry–gap associations among sampled optimized conformers; they do not establish torsional barriers, dynamics or spin-conversion rates.
